# Privacy basics

## Encryption

This can get heady, but I'm gonna keep it 100 for my non-technical comrades.
Encryption is a fancy word that you can think of as meaning "locked up." When we
encrypt anything - a message, a bit of text, an image, a video - we make it
impossible to read. The only way to see the encrypted message is to decrypt it,
aka "unlock it."

Encryption is a mechanism that works behind the scenes on some apps/programs.
These days, you don't gotta think about how to actually use encryption if you
don't wanna. (If you do wanna, I have another [post about that]()).

When you use an encrypted messaging app/program, all the things you say in those
messages are only readable by you and the homie(s) you send them to.

Encryption is used in password managers, VPNs, and so various messaging apps/
programs. I go into each of these in the following sections.

## VPNs and why you should be using one

VPN stands for Virtual Private Tunnel. Put very simply, it's a thingy that you
connect to that then connects you to the rest of the internet. The connection
you have with your VPN provider is encrypted, so all the data that passes
between you and it are secure and safe. When anyone tries to look up where the
web traffic came from, all they will see is that it came from a VPN provider.
The "who" is hidden. 

This is very important. Remember that sea of data I mentioned that you produce?
Now it can't be tied to _you_. It can only be tied to a company. Hopefully it's
a company that has values your privacy. And yes, there are some that do. Here's
